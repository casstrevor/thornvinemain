# Thornvine voice

A person sitting with you, not a form. Confident, warm, plain, and hopeful. Use contractions. Say "you" and "we." Keep sentences short. Leave out intake, stage, and field jargon on anything a client reads.

Authorized by Trevor, 2026-10-07, from the brand voice in `document/site/brand.md`.

## A question page

Each question uses the same three parts.

- **Eyebrow.** A short label. Not a sentence, and no period. It names the same thing as the title.
- **Title.** One question, or one statement on a review. Do not pack the choices into the title.
- **Support.** One sentence. It tells the person how to answer, or what the choices mean. It does not ask a second question.

Paired questions share a pattern.

- Eyebrows: "Who it's for" and "What it's for"
- Titles: "Who's this for?" and "What's this for?"

## Choices

Options in one group use the same grammar. Short phrases a person would say.

The unsure option is "Not sure yet" everywhere. "Something else" is the free-text option.

When more than one answer is welcome, the support is "Choose any that fit. More than one is welcome." When only one answer fits, the support is "Choose the one that fits." Skip that line when the support is already explaining the choices.

## Actions and status

Actions are sentence case: Next, Back, Return, Save and exit, Send for review.

Errors stay human. "Something snagged" is the title. The body says what is still safe.

Do not say "Stage 2" to a client. Say "the next conversation."
