## Purpose and positioning

Thornvine is a human-centered creative product agency that makes ambitious ideas approachable and achievable. Full-stack product development leads; AI, automation, design, and production support that promise.

Purpose: **We help humans turn imaginations into realities.**

Founders: **Luke and Trevor**, presented as equal co-founders and collaborators. Thornvine is their shared agency; do not describe it as Luke's solo business. The 30+ years of experience is combined team experience. Trevor's surname, individual bio, and photo remain to be supplied; do not invent them.

Voice: confident, playful, capable, honest, welcoming, collaborative, hopeful. Explain services plainly. Demonstrate humanity through real people, transparent process, and visible craftsmanship.

Working rules for product UI live in `apps/web/src/design-system/voice.md` (Trevor, 2026-10-07). Eyebrow, title, and support share one shape. Paired questions match: "Who it's for" / "What it's for", and "Who's this for?" / "What's this for?" The unsure choice is "Not sure yet." Clients do not see "Stage 2."

## Hero copy — working draft

- Eyebrow: Creative product agency
- Headline: **Your imagination. Let's make it real.**
- Body: We design and build custom apps, digital tools, and experiences for people with ideas. Bring your vision, your business challenge, or your "what if." We'll help you figure it out—and bring it to life.
- Primary action: **Tell us your idea**
- Secondary action: Explore our work
- Supporting copy: Start with a project brief. Your first consultation is free.

## Visual direction

- Warm ivory hero; bold left-aligned copy; sculptural tree on the right.
- Forest green, moss, warm cream, restrained burgundy; bright leaf green used sparingly for interaction.
- Bold Poppins headings and comfortable body type. Exact tokens remain to be defined.
- Glossy leaves, natural bark, subtle translucent surfaces; fine botanical lines and occasional art-deco framing.
- Scroll progression: cream, pale sage, forest green, deep woodland. The services section must blend continuously into the dark selected-work section through a broad gradient, without a hard border or abrupt color switch (Luke's refinement, 2026-09-29).
- Leaves gently tilt or drift with the pointer; the tree responds subtly. Never obstruct content, controls, or reading.
- Mobile: simplified scene, fewer leaves, comfortable spacing. Reduced motion: composed still scene. Content and conversion must work without 3D.
- Technical scene implementation is pending Cursor's baseline and proposal; visual direction does not prove any library is installed.

## Service groups

- **Digital products:** custom web/mobile apps, customer portals, internal tools, prototypes. Lead offering.
- **Design and strategy:** discovery, UX/UI, branding, shaping rough ideas into buildable plans.
- **AI and automation:** AI features, agents, connected workflows, business task automation.
- **Learning and creative:** training, onboarding, education, video, launch assets.

Use recognizable examples, such as a lawn-care portal, tattoo consultation tool, or interactive employee onboarding. Label illustrative concepts honestly; do not imply delivered client work.

## Page narrative

1. Your idea can become real — hero, tree, clear offering, brief CTA.
2. What we can create together — four service groups and concrete examples.
3. Explore the work — candidates: Wyldtracks, OVRmaps, Luke Libby's portfolio. Confirm links, permissions, role, and accurate descriptions before publishing.
4. Meet the humans — Luke and Trevor, with equal prominence, real founder photos, combined experience, curiosity, and collaboration. User reports 30+ years combined experience; confirm final public wording and founder bios.
5. See how it happens — free consultation, scoped proposal, visible milestones and working previews, launch and agreed support.
6. Tell us what you're imagining — welcoming project brief on deep forest background.

## Commercial clarity

Proposals should define deliverables, ownership/account access, anticipated running costs, maintenance scope and duration, and support terms. Website copy must not promise unlimited maintenance or unsupported outcomes.
